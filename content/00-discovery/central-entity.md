# Central Entity & Source Context — Crafty Puns

**Central Entity (CE):** Puns (wordplay)

**Source Context (SC):** Crafty Puns is a wordplay library — a site that organizes
original puns by topic and by use case, and gives visitors a free Pun Generator to
create their own. It monetizes through on-site advertising and, longer-term, the
Pun Generator as a retained-usage tool. The brand's reason to exist in this SERP is
that competing pun sites (Punpedia, Punscrazy) either mix puns with unrelated jokes/
slang, or bury topic-specific puns inside long undifferentiated lists. Crafty Puns'
edge is per-topic depth (one URL per topic, not one page for everything) plus a
generator tool competitors don't have.

**Bridge attribute:** topic + use (a pun exists *about* something and is written
*for* a specific use — a caption, a card, a kid, a joke).

**Central Search Intent (CSI):** CE + SC → "**find / make** puns about **[topic]**
for **[use]**." The functional predicates that recur across the whole map are
*find* (browse existing puns) and *make* (generate your own), always anchored to a
topic (cat, pizza, Christmas...) and often qualified by a use (captions, kids, love,
birthday, pickup lines).

**Sitewide n-grams:** "puns", "pun", "wordplay", "one-liners", "puns for [use]",
"[topic] puns", "pun generator", "double meaning".

**CORE definition:** Topic pun pages (the Topical Map sheet) — pages that unite the
CE (puns) with the SC (an organized, topic-specific wordplay library plus a
generator). These are CraftyPuns' highest-depth, most-linked-to pages.

**OUTER definition:** Use-hub pages (Puns for Kids, Love Puns, etc.) and the Outer
Section (What Is a Pun?, Types of Puns, etc.) — pages covering the CE plus a single
predicate of the CSI (one *use*, or the definitional/meta layer around puns
themselves) rather than the full topic+use matrix. They feed session and query data
back to the topic pages and link heavily into CORE.

**Monetisation path:** Display ads sitewide; the Pun Generator (linked from every
CORE page's "Make Your Own [X] Pun" block) is the retained-usage product that turns
one-time visitors into repeat visitors.

**Query template (topic pages):** every topic page answers the same reusable
question set: one-liners → short → funny → cute → captions → kids → love →
birthday → pickup lines → jokes → how to use → FAQ → related. This is the
**document template** (§5.4 of the framework) reused across all topic-page
entities, so coverage stays efficient even where a specific topic has lower
demand for one of the sub-questions (e.g. "Tea Love Puns" — still included for
template consistency and internal-link coverage).
