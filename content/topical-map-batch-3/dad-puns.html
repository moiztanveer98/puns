How to Use Dad Puns

Dad puns work well for Father’s Day cards, birthday gifts, and mugs or t-shirts; pair the appreciation puns with a genuine memory for cards, and save the funny dad-life lines for casual captions.

FAQ

What makes a joke a ’dad pun’?
Yes, a dad pun usually leans on a simple, groan-worthy wordplay delivered with total confidence, which is exactly what makes it funny.

Are dad puns good for Father’s Day gifts?
Yes, a short dad pun printed on a mug or t-shirt makes a fun, personal Father’s Day gift that plays into the joke rather than against it.

How are dad puns different from dad jokes?
Dad puns specifically rely on wordplay, while dad jokes can include any simple, corny joke format, puns included.
