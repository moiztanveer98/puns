How to Use Baseball Puns

Baseball puns work well on scorecards, team merch, ballpark food captions, and fantasy league names; the ballpark-themed lines are especially good for opening-day social posts.

FAQ

What’s a good pun for a baseball team?
Yes, puns built around ’pitch,’ ’batter,’ or ’home run’ make strong team names since they reference the actual game every player and fan knows.

Are baseball puns good for scorecards?
Yes, a short baseball pun scribbled in the margin of a scorecard is a classic way fans add personality to a long game.

Can I use baseball puns for a fantasy league name?
Yes, baseball puns make especially memorable fantasy league and team names, particularly when built around a player’s name.
