FAQ

What’s a good pun for a Father’s Day card?
Yes, puns built around grilling, tools, or classic dad jokes work especially well since they tie directly into things most dads actually do.

Are dad jokes the same as Father’s Day puns?
Not exactly, dad jokes are a broader style of corny humor, while Father’s Day puns are specifically built around the holiday and fatherhood themes.

How do I write a funny Father’s Day caption?
Pair a short pun about grilling, tools, or dad jokes with one genuine, specific line about what makes your dad great.
