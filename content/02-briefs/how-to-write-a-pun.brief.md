## Contextual structure

- **Intro**: sentence 1 states the 5 steps exist and what they produce (an
  original pun, not a recycled one). Sentence 2 previews the steps in order,
  matching the H2s exactly.
- **Each step (H2)**: imperative instructional format — verb-first sentence,
  one worked example carried through all 5 steps using the SAME example topic
  (e.g. "coffee") so the reader sees one pun built start to finish across the
  whole page. Each step ends with a one-sentence hint toward the next step.
- **Step 3 (border)**: explicitly names the two mechanisms (sound-alike /
  double meaning) and links to the matching type pages using "homophonic" and
  "homographic" as anchor text.
- **Good vs. Bad (H3, comparative)**: contrasts one strong and one weak
  version of the running coffee-pun example, links to Bad Puns for the
  deliberately-bad-on-purpose counter-case.
- **Try the Pun Generator (H2)**: 1–2 sentence CTA bridge, no new instruction.
