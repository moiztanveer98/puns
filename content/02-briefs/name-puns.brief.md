## Word bank

This page is structurally different from other topic pages: instead of one fixed
vocabulary, each pun plays on a specific common first name (Sam, Jack, Will, Grace,
Max, Rose, etc.) and its sound-alike or meaning. Quoted terms are the example names
themselves — use at least 15 distinct names across the page, no name repeated.
